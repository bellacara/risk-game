How to play: You and the automated player can hold exactly four cards. On your turn, draw one card from the central deck, then choose one of your five cards to discard. The automated player does the same, but its cards remain hidden. The first player to hold a four-of-a-kind wins. Jokers are wild.

A tactical card game · No accounts, downloads, or tracking.
